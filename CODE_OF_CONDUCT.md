# Code of Conduct

ingresso is built in the open. We want this repository to be welcoming to
everyone who contributes to it.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct for the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions.

## In short

- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or sexual language or imagery.
- Don't publish anyone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@ingresso.dev**. Reports are handled in confidence, and we will not
tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors,
following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
