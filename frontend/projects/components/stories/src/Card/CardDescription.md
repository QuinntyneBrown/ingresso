A card is a bordered surface that groups related content, such as a settings section or a form block. Put it on the element whose semantics fit (`<section ig-card>`, `<article ig-card>`) or use `<ig-card>`. `title` renders the heading and labels the card; `[slot=actions]` sits beside the title and `[slot=footer]` closes the card. `flush` removes the padding for edge-to-edge content, `interactive` lifts the card on hover and `selected` gives it the accent border. App tiles are their own component.
