A menu is a dropdown of three or more related actions behind one trigger, such as the account menu with Settings and Sign out. Put `igMenuTriggerFor` on a button and point it at an `<ng-template>` holding an `ig-menu`. Angular CDK Menu supplies the overlay, `aria-haspopup` and `aria-expanded`, roving focus, ↑/↓, Home/End, typeahead and Escape, and returns focus to the trigger on close. Items are `button[ig-menu-item]` or `a[ig-menu-item]`; `kind="danger"` marks a destructive item, `shortcut` shows a key hint and `disabled` sets `aria-disabled`. `ig-menu-divider` separates groups.
