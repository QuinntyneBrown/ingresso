Tabs switch between two to five peer views of the same data on one page. Each `ig-tab-panel` gives its tab a `label` (and optional `count` badge) and is the `role="tabpanel"` region, labelled by its tab. `ig-tabs` renders the `role="tablist"` from the panels, named by `label`. Activation is automatic: ← and → move to and select the next tab, Home and End jump to the first and last, and Tab moves into the panel. Only the selected tab is in the tab order. `selectedIndex` is two-way bindable.
