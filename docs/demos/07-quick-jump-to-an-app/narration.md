## 01-dashboard
When you have many apps, finding one should not take a hunt. Here is the dashboard.

## 02-search-focused
Press the slash key, anywhere, and the search is ready. The slash itself is not typed.

## 03-typed-first-match-highlighted
Type a few letters, and the tiles narrow down by name, address, category and description. The first match is highlighted, and the number of matches is announced for screen readers.

## 04-highlight-moved
Use the arrow keys to move the highlight. At the end, it wraps around to the other side.

## 05-app-opened
Press Enter, and the highlighted app opens in a new tab, and ingresso notes that you opened it.

## 06-no-match
Search for something that is not there, and ingresso says so plainly, with a way to clear the search. Press Escape, and you are right back where you were. A slash, a few letters, Enter, and you are in.
