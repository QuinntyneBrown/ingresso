## 01-your-devices
See and end other sessions shows you everywhere you're signed in. In Settings, Devices lists each one by browser and system, when it was last active, and when it signed in. This computer is marked as this device, and here's your phone too.

## 02-end-session-asks-first
Don't recognise one, or left yourself signed in somewhere? Choose End session. ingresso asks first and names the device, so you end the right one.

## 03-session-ended
End it, and a toast confirms it. Only this device is left. Apps you signed in to on the other device can't renew their access either. And when there are several, Sign out of all other devices ends them all at once.

## 04-the-phone-is-signed-out
On the phone, the next step goes straight back to sign-in. You stay in charge of every place you're signed in.
