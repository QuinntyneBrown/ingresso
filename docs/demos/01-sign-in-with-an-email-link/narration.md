## 01-sign-in
Sign in with an email link lets you into ingresso without a password to remember. You start with one question: what is your email address?

## 02-check-your-inbox
Type it in and ask for a link. ingresso answers the same way for everyone, so nobody can use this page to find out who has an account. The link works once, and only for fifteen minutes.

## 03-private-dashboard
Open the link from your inbox, and you are in. This is your own dashboard, and only you can see the apps on it.

## 04-link-already-used
Try the same link again, and ingresso says it was already used. Nobody can borrow it, and you can simply ask for a fresh one. In short: one email, one link, one door, and it is yours alone.
