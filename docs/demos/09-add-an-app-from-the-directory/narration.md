## 01-browse-directory-in-add-dialog
Adding an app from the directory lets you pick a common app in one click, instead of typing its name and address. Open Add app, and choose Browse directory.

## 02-directory-with-added-badges
Here are common apps, grouped by category, each with its icon and a short description. The ones already on your dashboard say Added, so you never add the same app twice by accident.

## 03-search-narrows-the-list
Start typing, and the list narrows to the apps that match.

## 04-form-pre-filled
Choose Add, and the form opens with the name, address, description and category already filled in. Change anything you like.

## 05-new-tile-on-the-dashboard
Save, and the new tile lands in the first free space on your dashboard. That's adding an app from the directory: browse, search, add.
