## 01-the-activity-card
Review sign-in activity shows you what has happened on your ingresso account. In Settings, a new Activity card sits below Devices. Choose View activity.

## 02-your-activity
Activity lists your security events, newest first: each sign-in with the browser and system it came from, apps you approved, denied or revoked, and sessions you ended. Here's the phone session ended a moment ago. ingresso keeps events for ninety days, then deletes them.

## 03-only-sign-ins
Looking for something in particular? Choose a filter. Sign-ins shows only sign-ins, to ingresso and to your apps. If one came from a device you don't recognise, end that session in Devices.

## 04-apps-and-keys
Administrators get one more filter. Apps and keys lists every app registered, edited, disabled or deleted, every secret and signing key rotated, and who did it. This app was registered from the command line. Members never see these, only their own events.
