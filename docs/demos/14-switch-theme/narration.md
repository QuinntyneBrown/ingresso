## 01-dashboard-in-light
Switch theme lets ingresso look the way you like. By default it follows your device, and right now that's light.

## 02-settings
Open the account menu from your initials, and choose Settings. Under Appearance you can match your system, or pick Light or Dark.

## 03-dark-chosen
Choose Dark, and it changes right away. There's no save button to press.

## 04-dashboard-in-dark
Head back to your apps, and the whole dashboard has followed. The text stays easy to read in both themes.

## 05-kept-after-reload
Reload the page and it's still dark. Your choice is remembered on this device. That's switching theme: pick it once, and it stays.
