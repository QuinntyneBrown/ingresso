## 01-dashboard-light
Meet the ingresso app shell. It is the front door to the apps you build and use. Every app is a tile on your dashboard, and a search box at the top gets you to any of them in a keystroke.

## 02-dashboard-dark
If you prefer a darker screen, ingresso follows your device. The colors change, but the layout, the contrast and the tiles stay just as clear.

## 03-phone-header
On a phone, the header stacks neatly. Your actions sit on their own row, the search fills the width below, and every control is big enough for a thumb.

## 04-not-found
Follow a broken link and ingresso tells you plainly. The page may have moved, and one button takes you straight back to your apps.

## 05-app-added-toast
Add an app, and a small message confirms it. It is announced to screen readers too, so everyone knows the app is on the dashboard. In short, the ingresso shell is fast, readable on any screen, and kind when something goes wrong.
