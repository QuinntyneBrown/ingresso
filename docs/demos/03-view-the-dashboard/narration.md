## 01-loading
Open ingresso, and while your apps are on their way, you see where they will go. Soft placeholders hold the space, so nothing jumps around when they arrive.

## 02-full-dashboard
Here is your dashboard. Every app sits exactly where you left it, at the size you chose, with its name, its address and its colour.

## 03-tablet
On a tablet, the tiles flow into two tidy columns, in the same reading order. Nothing is moved or saved, so your layout is safe.

## 04-phone
On a phone, they stack in one column, and everything is still within a thumb's reach.

## 05-empty-state
And if you have no apps yet, ingresso invites you to add the first one. In short: your whole front door, at a glance, on any screen.
