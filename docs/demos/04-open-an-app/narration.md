## 01-dashboard
Here is your dashboard. Each tile is a door to one of your apps.

## 02-app-in-new-tab
Click a tile, or press Enter on it, and the app opens in a new tab. The page you open cannot reach back into ingresso, so your dashboard stays private.

## 03-back-on-dashboard
Switch back, and the tile now says it was just opened. If recording that ever fails, the app still opens, and nothing gets in your way. One click, one new tab, and you are in.
