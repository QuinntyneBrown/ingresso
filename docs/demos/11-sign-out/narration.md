## 01-account-menu-open
Signing out lets you leave ingresso safely on any device. Open the account menu from your initials in the header, and choose Sign out.

## 02-confirm-sign-out
A quick check first. Your tiles stay saved, and you'll sign in again with an emailed link next time. Choose Sign out to confirm, or stay signed in.

## 03-back-at-sign-in
That's it. Your session is ended on the server, so the old one can never be used again, and you're back at sign in.
