## 01-hi-there
Greet by profile name means ingresso welcomes you by the name you choose, never by your email address. A brand-new account hasn't chosen one yet, so the dashboard simply says Hi there, with a small Add your name link underneath.

## 02-add-your-name
Choose Add your name, and Edit your profile opens right over your apps. Type the name you'd like to see. Your email stays as it is.

## 03-hi-sam
Save changes, and the greeting changes at once: Hi, Sam Rivera. No reload, and the Add your name link is gone because you don't need it any more.

## 04-on-a-phone
On a phone the greeting fits the screen. Even a long name wraps onto more lines instead of running off the side.

## 05-settings-profile
In Settings, the Profile card shows your display name and, separately, your email. Change or clear the name here whenever you like, and the dashboard follows.

## 06-signed-in-again
Sign out, sign back in with an emailed link, and ingresso still greets you by name. That's greet by profile name: your name, your way, every time you come back.
