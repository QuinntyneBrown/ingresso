## 01-dashboard
Your dashboard is yours to arrange. Every tile has a place and a size.

## 02-edit-layout-mode
Choose Edit layout, and the grid lines appear, with a bar that says how it works. Each tile gets a handle, a corner to resize, and an Edit button.

## 03-dragging-to-free-cells
Drag a tile, and a shadow shows exactly where it will land.

## 04-resized
Drag its corner to make it bigger, within the limits of the grid.

## 05-drop-refused
Try to drop it on another tile, and the shadow warns you. The tile goes back, nothing else moves, and nothing is saved.

## 06-moved-with-keyboard
No mouse? Use the arrow keys to move a tile, and hold Shift to resize. Every change is announced.

## 07-done
Choose Done, and your layout is already saved. Every change saves as you go, so there is nothing left to lose.
