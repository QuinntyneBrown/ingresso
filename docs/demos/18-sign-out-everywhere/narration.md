## 01-signed-in-to-the-app
Sign out everywhere means one sign-out is enough. Here you're signed in to the sample app with ingresso, and to ingresso itself on the same device.

## 02-sign-out-of-ingresso
Sign out of ingresso from the account menu. The dialog tells you that apps you signed in to with ingresso are signed out too.

## 03-signed-out-of-every-app
ingresso ends your session, then quietly asks each of those apps to end theirs. When they're done, it lists the apps you've been signed out of.

## 04-the-app-is-signed-out
Back in the sample app, you're signed out. Nothing was left behind.

## 05-ingresso-is-signed-out-too
It works the other way round as well. Sign in again, sign out in the app, and ingresso's session ends with it, so ingresso asks you to sign in next time. One sign-out, every app.
