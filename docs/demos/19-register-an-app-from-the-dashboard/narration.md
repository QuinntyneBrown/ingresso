## 01-make-an-administrator
Register an app from the dashboard lets you set up the apps that sign in with ingresso without opening a terminal every time. First, one command makes you an administrator. That's the only thing the command line has to do.

## 02-settings-apps
Now Settings has an Apps card. It lists every app that signs in with ingresso, with its type and client ID. People who aren't administrators never see it.

## 03-register-a-confidential-app
Choose Register an app and fill in the details: its name, where to send people after they sign in and sign out, and the APIs it can call. This one runs on a server, so it's confidential and gets a secret.

## 04-the-secret-shown-once
Here's the secret. ingresso shows it this once and keeps only a scrambled copy, so copy it into the app now. The copy button confirms it worked.

## 05-the-new-app-in-the-list
And Budget is in the list, ready to sign people in. Become an administrator once, then register every app right from Settings.
