## 01-rearranged-in-edit-layout
Reset the layout puts your dashboard back in order whenever you've moved things around. Here, in edit layout mode, you can drag, resize and nudge tiles anywhere. When you want a fresh start, choose Reset layout in the bar.

## 02-confirm-reset
A quick check first. Positions and sizes will change, but no app is deleted or edited. Choose Reset layout to go ahead, or Keep my layout to change nothing.

## 03-layout-reset
Done. Every app is back in the order you added it, four to a row, and a short message confirms it. That's the whole reset.
