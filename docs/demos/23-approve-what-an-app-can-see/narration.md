## 01-the-sample-app
Approve what an app can see puts you in charge of your apps. Here's an app that signs in with ingresso. It wants more than just your sign-in.

## 02-what-the-app-will-see
So before it gets anything, ingresso asks you. The page names the app and lists, in plain words, what it will be able to see and do: your display name, your email address, its own API, and staying signed in.

## 03-allowed-back-in-the-app
Choose Allow and you're straight back in the app, signed in. Choose Deny and the app is told no, with nothing shared.

## 04-not-asked-again
ingresso remembers your choice. Sign in to the same app again and it goes straight through. If the app later asks for something new, you're asked about that alone.

## 05-first-party-apps-skip-it
For apps you run yourself, an administrator can mark them first-party, in Settings or from the command line, and they skip the page. Everyone else asks first.
