## 01-settings-profile
Share profile details with apps lets you choose how your apps know you. Settings now has a Profile card with your display name and your email, the details your apps can see when they ask.

## 02-edit-your-profile
Choose Edit profile and type the name you'd like to go by. Your email is shown too, but it can't be changed here. Leave the name empty and your email is used instead.

## 03-profile-saved
Save it, and a toast confirms it. The card shows your new name right away, and so does your greeting at the top of ingresso.

## 04-the-sample-app
Now open an app that signs in with ingresso. This sample app asks for your profile and your email when you sign in.

## 05-greeted-by-name
And there you are. The app greets you by the name you just chose, because it asked for your profile and ingresso shared it.

## 06-userinfo-by-scope
Apps only get what they ask for. Ask for profile and email, and the app sees your name and your verified address. Ask for neither, and it sees only an anonymous id. Your name, shared where you choose.
