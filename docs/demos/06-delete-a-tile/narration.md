## 01-edit-layout-mode
When you no longer use an app, remove it. Turn on edit layout, and open Edit on the tile you want gone.

## 02-confirmation-names-the-tile
Choose Delete, and ingresso asks first. It names the app, so you know exactly what you are about to remove, and it tells you the app itself is not touched. Keep the app, and nothing changes.

## 03-tile-gone-with-toast
Confirm, and the tile disappears, with a message to say so. Every other tile stays right where it was. A clean dashboard is one confirmation away.
