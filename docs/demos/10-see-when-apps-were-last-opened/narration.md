## 01-every-tile-says-when-it-was-opened
Seeing when apps were last opened tells you at a glance what you use and what you have not touched. Each tile says when its app was last opened, or that it never was.

## 02-open-an-app
Choose a tile to open its app in a new tab.

## 03-opened-just-now
Back on your dashboard, the tile now says it was opened just now. Hover the time for the exact moment, and screen readers hear the app's name along with it.
