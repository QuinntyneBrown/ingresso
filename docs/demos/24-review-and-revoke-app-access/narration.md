## 01-signed-in-to-the-app
Review and revoke app access keeps you in charge after you've said yes. Here you've signed in to the sample app and allowed it to use your ingresso account.

## 02-connected-apps
In Settings, Connected apps lists every app you've allowed, and only yours. Each one says, in plain words, what it can do, when you approved it, and when it last used your sign-in. ingresso works that out from the tokens it issued, without keeping anything the app did.

## 03-revoke-asks-first
Choose Revoke and ingresso asks first. It names the app and what it will lose, and tells you the app is signed out wherever it holds your sign-in.

## 04-access-revoked
Revoke access, and a toast confirms it. The app is gone from the list. Its tokens stop working straight away, for you only. Anyone else using the app stays signed in.

## 05-it-asks-again
The next time the app wants your sign-in, ingresso asks you again, from the start. You decide afresh what it can see.
