Link

Links go somewhere: another page or an app in a new tab.

Overview

Links are underlined text. Inside tiles the whole tile is the link (see App tile); footer links and the back link are standalone links.

Anatomy

  1. TextDescribes the destination
  2. UnderlineAlways on; the cue beyond colour

Inline and standalone with an icon.

Responsive behaviour

Fluid: the component fills its container and needs no breakpoint of its own. Verified at 360, 768 and 1280px.

Theming

Link reads semantic tokens only (listed under Tokens), so it follows the theme without extra CSS. To re-theme it, override its component tokens or the semantic tokens on a [data-theme] ancestor.
