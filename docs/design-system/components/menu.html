Menu

A dropdown of actions opened from a button, such as the account menu.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use a menu for three or more related actions behind one trigger. The account menu holds Settings and Sign out.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerA button with aria-haspopup
  2. Items32px tall, icon optional
  3. DividerSeparates destructive items

Variants

Standard and danger items.

Responsive behaviour

Fluid: the component fills its container and needs no breakpoint of its own. Verified at 360, 768 and 1280px.

Theming

Menu reads semantic tokens only (listed under Tokens), so it follows the theme without extra CSS. To re-theme it, override its component tokens or the semantic tokens on a [data-theme] ancestor.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA Menu Button: trigger has aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded; the list is role="menu" with menuitem children.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter, Space, ↓Open and focus the first item
↑ / ↓Move between items
Home / EndFirst / last item
Type a letterJump to a matching item
EscClose and return focus to the trigger

Focus

Focus moves into the menu on open and returns to the trigger on close.

Labelling

The trigger is labelled ("Account menu for Maya Reyes").

Motion

Scales in over --duration-fast.

Touch

Items are 32px; the trigger is 44px on phones.

Content

  • Items are verbs or destinations.
  • Put destructive items last, after a divider.
