Pagination

Move between pages of a long list.

Responsive behaviour

Fluid: the component fills its container and needs no breakpoint of its own. Verified at 360, 768 and 1280px.

Theming

Pagination reads semantic tokens only (listed under Tokens), so it follows the theme without extra CSS. To re-theme it, override its component tokens or the semantic tokens on a [data-theme] ancestor.
