Divider

A hairline that separates groups.

Overview

Use sparingly: spacing separates most things. Dividers split alternatives (the "or" between sign-in options) and a tile footer from its body.

Sizes

Divider has a single size.

States

A divider has no interactive states.

Responsive behaviour

Fluid: the component fills its container and needs no breakpoint of its own. Verified at 360, 768 and 1280px.

Theming

Divider reads semantic tokens only (listed under Tokens), so it follows the theme without extra CSS. To re-theme it, override its component tokens or the semantic tokens on a [data-theme] ancestor.
