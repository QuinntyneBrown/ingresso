Alert

A message inside the page: inline or as a page-wide banner.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use for state the person needs while they work: load failed, offline, session expiring. Banners span the page and stay until the state ends.

Anatomy

  1. IconTone
  2. TitleWhat happened
  3. MessageWhat to do
  4. ActionsRetry or dismiss

Variants

Four tones; inline and banner.

Sizes

Alert has a single size.

States

Alerts have no interactive states of their own; actions inside use Button states.

Responsive behaviour

Fluid: the component fills its container and needs no breakpoint of its own. Verified at 360, 768 and 1280px.

Theming

Alert reads semantic tokens only (listed under Tokens), so it follows the theme without extra CSS. To re-theme it, override its component tokens or the semantic tokens on a [data-theme] ancestor.
