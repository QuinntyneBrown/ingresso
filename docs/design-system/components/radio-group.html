Radio group

Overview

Use for two to five options where seeing all of them helps. The Appearance setting (System, Light, Dark) is a radio group.

Anatomy

  2. Radio20px circle

Responsive behaviour

Fluid: the component fills its container and needs no breakpoint of its own. Verified at 360, 768 and 1280px.

Theming

Radio group reads semantic tokens only (listed under Tokens), so it follows the theme without extra CSS. To re-theme it, override its component tokens or the semantic tokens on a [data-theme] ancestor.
