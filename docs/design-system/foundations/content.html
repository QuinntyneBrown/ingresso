Overview

ingresso sounds like a good doorman: brief, warm, and clear about what happens next. "Every app, one door."

Voice and casing

  • Sentence case for headings, buttons and labels. The wordmark is lowercase: ingresso.
  • Verbs on buttons: Add app, Save changes, Delete Linear. Never "OK" or "Submit".
  • Say "app" and "tile" consistently: an app is the thing; a tile is how it appears.

Numbers and times

  • Relative times for opens: "Opened 8 minutes ago".
  • Counts include the noun: "11 apps".
  • Addresses are shown without the scheme, in Geist Mono.

Error message formula

What happened, then what to do. No blame, no jargon.

DoDon't
We couldn’t load your apps. Check your connection and try again.Error 500: request failed.
That email and password don’t match.Invalid credentials.

Empty-state formula

Title says what is missing; one sentence says why it helps; one primary action. "No apps behind this door yet — Add the apps you build and use, and they show up here as tiles. [Add your first app]"
