Dialogs and overlays

When to use a dialog, drawer, popover or a page.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • When to use
  • Structure
  • States
  • Responsive behaviour
  • Accessibility
  • Content

Overview

Add, edit, delete and sign-out use dialogs. Anything larger than a short form becomes a page. Menus and tooltips are lightweight overlays.

When to use

NeedUse
Short form or confirmationDialog (CDK Dialog with DialogShell)
Long or multi-step taskPage
A few actions behind a buttonMenu
Name an iconTooltip

Structure

Delete Linear?

This removes the tile from your dashboard.

Live composed example

States

StateBehaviour
OpenFocus on the first field or the safest button; page inert
BusyConfirm busy; Close disabled; no second submit
FailedAlert at the top; entries kept
CloseEsc, close button or Cancel; focus returns to the opener

Responsive behaviour

Under 640px dialogs are bottom sheets; stacked dialogs are avoided (one at a time).

Accessibility

  • role="dialog" aria-modal="true", labelled and described.
  • Do not open a dialog from a dialog; navigate instead.

Content

  • Title is a question for confirmations.
  • Primary button repeats the verb and object.
